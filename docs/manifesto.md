\# Strawberry Canyon Manifesto



Markets are adaptive systems moving through interacting states.



The regime is the whole.

Signals are derived from it.



\---



\# State precedes signal



Price is not primary.



Context is primary.



The objective is not prediction.



The objective is recognizing:

\- propagation

\- exhaustion

\- roughness

\- persistence

\- instability

\- transition



\---



\# Trade state, not price



Positioning follows regime structure.



The framework prioritizes:

\- state

\- topology

\- structural location

\- propagation quality

\- cross-asset confirmation



over isolated directional prediction.



\---



\# Propagation matters more than direction



Volatility measures magnitude.



Fragility measures propagation behavior.



The interaction between:

\- FRAGILITY\_1530

\- FRAGILITY\_1930



creates temporal persistence topology.



\---



\# Observation is participatory



Markets cannot be observed from outside the system.



Observation and participation are inseparable.



Strawberry Canyon treats markets as participant-state environments rather than mechanical objects.



\---



\# Functional contextualism



Indicators are not universally true or false.



They are functional or non-functional relative to:

\- context

\- regime

\- objective

\- structural state



Truth is contextual fitness.
